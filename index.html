मंगलदीप

Mangal Deep Vidya Mandir, the “Lamp of Goodness” school

A school for children with special needs in Almora, Uttarakhand, since 1998. We teach and train children who are mentally challenged, hearing impaired, autistic or spastic, who would otherwise have no school to go to in these hills.

Why Mangaldeep exists

Before 1998, children with special needs in and around Almora had no suitable facilities. Most came from poor households and were often left to fend for themselves, and regular schools could not meet their needs. A group of educationists set up Mangal Deep Vidya Mandir to give these children a place to learn.

Mission

To provide opportunities to children with different abilities to develop to their potential and achieve independence to the extent possible, in a caring, friendly and professional environment.

Our beliefs

  • Children with disabilities and their families have a right to dignity, respect and opportunity.
  • Each child’s experience of disability is unique, and each child can learn and grow.
  • Education should be child and family driven.

Vision

Children acquire the basic competencies and support they need to access appropriate education or skills, and to participate as functional and, as far as possible, independent members of society.

Products made by our students

Students make these as part of their vocational training. Each purchase helps them earn while they learn, and move toward independence.
